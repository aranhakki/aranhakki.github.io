I believe the concept of "observation" is the single most important aspect of complex systems thinking, which can be applied to many different aspects of problem solving throughout life and technical fields.
Observation is simply the act of observing, where one system observes another, where a system is just a named entity associated with a set of behaviours and interactions.
Lets consider a recursive algorithm acting on a recursive data structure. By running or analysing the algorithm we can observe its behaviour, each approach is a means of observation. 
If we were to perform a proof by induction over the recursive algorithm, we would be performing an other type of observation, which would be categorized as a formal deductive method.
Various types of observation are just ways to view, analyse and model various systems, all which are useful for different purposes.
Choosing one over the other has a huge impact in the way in which we can perceive, analyse and understand problems. 
As far as we known humans have the greatest ability to be conscientious observers, i.e. be observers with the highest level of consciousness. 
In general observation, means observing energy flows, this could be traditional physics measures of energy, or other indirect measures like money, happiness, stress or whatever one would like to model.
Design of optimal systems can be viewed as designing systems that can be observed to interact with other systems, such that the observed energy flows are regenerative, ideally growing in some positive aspect.
E.g. lets consider an economic system in which we can maximise the happiness of all involved, and design the system such that the happiness would grow over time, this would be an ideallized successful economic system.
A system that does not regenerate, will eventual run out of energy, and hence will cease to exist. This means that systems never exist in isolation, there must always be some kind of co-dependence or symbiotic behaviour involved.
